Privacy Policy

For the Discord application operated by an individual developer and used together with WorkBuddy （以下称 “本应用”）
Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data this Application (the “App”) processes, why, and what choices you have. The App is a personal, non-commercial Discord application that connects Discord with the operator’s WorkBuddy workspace. The App is not an official product of Discord Inc. or of WorkBuddy.

2. Data the App Processes

The App only processes data that is necessary for it to respond to an interaction:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, usernames, server (guild) IDs, and channel IDs attached to a command, mention, or other interaction directed at the App.
  • Message content you direct at the App: the text of slash commands, messages that mention the App, or messages in a channel/thread you explicitly use to interact with the App. The App does not read or store messages that are not directed at it.
  • Technical data: timestamps and basic interaction metadata needed to process a request, prevent abuse, and diagnose errors.

The App does not collect your Discord password, payment information, or data from servers it has not been installed in.

3. How Data Is Used

  • To carry out your request: the content you direct at the App is forwarded to the operator’s WorkBuddy workspace / connected AI service so a response can be generated and returned to the same Discord channel.
  • To operate, secure, and debug the App (for example, short-lived error logs).

The App does not sell personal data, does not use it for advertising, and does not build user profiles across servers.

4. Storage and Retention

The App is designed to process interactions in transit. Message content is not permanently stored by the App; temporary processing data and error logs, where they exist, are kept only as long as needed for operation and troubleshooting and are then deleted. If you interact with the App through WorkBuddy or an AI service, that service processes the forwarded content under its own privacy policy.

5. Sharing of Data

Data is shared only with the services strictly required to fulfil a request: Discord (to deliver messages), and the operator’s WorkBuddy workspace / connected AI provider (to generate a response). Data may also be disclosed if required by law.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Stop using the App: remove the App from your server, or simply stop directing messages at it; no further content will be processed.
  • Access / deletion requests: you may ask the operator what data (if any) relating to you is retained and request its deletion by opening an issue in the project repository: github.com/mantou2046/HorizonNews/issues. Requests will be handled within a reasonable time.

7. Security

The operator takes reasonable measures to protect the App’s credentials and any temporarily processed data, including keeping the bot token confidential and limiting the App’s Discord permissions to what its functions require. No system is completely secure, and the operator cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children below the minimum age required to use Discord in their country, and it does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will always be available at this URL, with the “Last updated” date revised accordingly.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be raised by opening an issue in the project repository: github.com/mantou2046/HorizonNews/issues.
